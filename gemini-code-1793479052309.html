Grammar Essentials

Lee las reglas principales. Usa las notas en español si lo necesitas.

1. Structure (Estructura)

Affirmative: Subject + have/has + past participle

I have tried sushi.

Negative: Subject + haven't/hasn't + past participle

She hasn't called me.

Question: Have/Has + subject + past participle?

Have you finished?

Remember: Use has with he/she/it. Recuerda: usa 'has' (o hasn't) solo para la tercera persona singular.

2. Past Participles

Regular verbs end in -ed. Irregular verbs change completely.

  • Work → worked (Regular)
  • Study → studied (Regular)
  • See → seen (Irregular)
  • Go → gone (Irregular)
Debes memorizar la tercera columna de los verbos irregulares.

3. Time Expressions (Expresiones de tiempo)

Since: Starting point (since 2020)
Desde un punto específico.
For: Duration (for 3 years)
Por un periodo de tiempo.
Ever: Any time (Have you ever...?)
¿Alguna vez...? (En preguntas)
Never: No time (I have never...)
Nunca. (Hace la oración negativa)
Already: Sooner than expected (I've already eaten)
Ya. (Acciones terminadas)
Yet: Until now (Not yet / finished yet?)
Todavía no / Ya (En negativas y preguntas).

4. Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

Use the Present Perfect for actions connected to the present or when the time is not specific. Use the Past Simple for finished actions in a specific time in the past.

Present Perfect: I have been to Paris. (Sometime in my life)

Past Simple: I went to Paris in 2019. (Finished time)
